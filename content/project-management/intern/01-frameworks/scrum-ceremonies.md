## Overview

**Scrum** is the most widely adopted Agile framework. Mastering the rhythm of Scrum events, roles, and artifact commitments is crucial for aspiring Associate Project Managers, Scrum Masters, and Project Coordinators.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                             The Scrum Lifecycle                               |
|                                                                               |
|  [ Product Backlog ] ──( Sprint Planning )──> [ Sprint Backlog ]              |
|                                                      │                        |
|                                         ( Daily Standups: 1-4 Weeks )         |
|                                                      │                        |
|                                                      ▼                        |
|  [ Sprint Retro ] <──( Sprint Review Demo )── [ Shipped Product Increment ]   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## The 3 Key Scrum Roles

1. **Product Owner (PO)**:
   - Maximizes product value; owns and prioritizes the Product Backlog; acts as the voice of business and customers.
2. **Scrum Master (SM)**:
   - Promotes and supports Scrum practices; acts as a servant leader; removes impediments/blockers for the team.
3. **Developers / Engineering Team**:
   - Cross-functional, self-organizing professionals who turn backlog items into working increments each sprint.

---

## The 5 Core Scrum Events

1. **The Sprint**: The heartbeat of Scrum; a time-boxed container event (usually 2 weeks) where ideas become value.
2. **Sprint Planning**: Collaboratively determines the Sprint Goal and selects candidate backlog items for the Sprint Backlog.
3. **Daily Scrum (Daily Standup)**: A 15-minute time-boxed synchronization meeting for developers to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and surface blockers.
4. **Sprint Review**: Held at the end of the sprint to demonstrate completed features to stakeholders and collect real-time feedback.
5. **Sprint Retrospective**: Dedicated team continuous improvement meeting focused on process optimization, team health, tools, and relationships.
